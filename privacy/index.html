English

Your library. Your choice.

Glint helps you review photos and videos in your system Photos library. This policy explains what the app handles, what stays on your device, and what happens when you choose a system service.

A simple, free app

All app features are free. Glint has no accounts, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising, or analytics SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Photos and their details

Glint uses Apple's PhotoKit to access only the photos and videos you authorize. It reads previews and available details such as dates, dimensions, file sizes, camera information, and location coordinates already stored with an item. It does not use your device's live location to track you.

The app processes your library choices on your device and does not upload your photos, videos, or their metadata to a developer server. iCloud Photos may download requested media through Apple's system services; the app is not a separate photo backup or cloud service.

What is saved on your device

Glint saves identifiers for queued and kept items, cleanup dates and counts, estimated file-size totals, and preferences such as group size and autoplay. These records let the app restore your queue, show your cleanup history, and remember your choices.

The app may create a temporary local export when you choose to share. Glint does not operate a server copy of your library or cleanup records. Deleting the app removes this installation's local app data; merely offloading the app or revoking Photos access is different from deleting its data.

Deletion and permission

Swiping left queues an item; it does not delete that item from Photos. Deletion occurs only after you review and confirm, using the system Photos service.

Deleted photos and videos normally remain in Recently Deleted for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions can sync to your other devices. Glint's file-size estimates do not guarantee an immediate increase in available storage.

You can reduce or revoke Photos access in system Settings. Revoking permission prevents further access; it does not delete your system photo library, remove previously saved app records, or undo deletions you already confirmed.

Maps and sharing are your actions

Glint opens Apple Maps for an item's existing coordinates only when you choose that action. It does not automatically request map images while you inspect an item.

Sharing opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination and whether to send the exported item. The app uses the current edited version when available; the shared file may include existing metadata. The destination app or service handles the data you choose to share under its own practices.

When you contact support

Support is provided through the public GitHub issue tracker linked below. If you open an issue, GitHub and visitors to that public repository can see your GitHub account name, message, and attachments. The app does not automatically send diagnostic reports or your photo library.

Do not post photos, videos, precise locations, or other sensitive information. You can edit or close your issue using GitHub's controls. GitHub handles the information under its own terms and privacy statement; Glint does not promise a fixed response deadline.

Updates and questions

The date on this page identifies this version of the policy. If the app's data handling changes, this page should be updated to reflect those changes. For questions about the app or support correspondence, use the operator and contact details below.

简体中文

你的相册，由你掌控。

拾晖帮助你浏览和整理系统相册里的照片与视频。这份说明介绍 App 会处理哪些信息、哪些记录保存在设备上，以及你主动使用系统服务时会发生什么。

简单、免费的整理工具

所有 App 功能免费使用。拾晖没有账号、订阅、内购、广告或分析统计 SDK，也不会跨 App 或网站追踪你。

照片与相关信息

拾晖通过 Apple 的 PhotoKit 访问你授权的照片与视频，读取预览和可取得的日期、尺寸、文件大小、相机参数，以及项目原本包含的位置坐标。App 不会使用设备实时定位来追踪你。

照片去留等整理操作在设备上处理。拾晖不会把照片、视频及其元数据上传到开发者服务器。iCloud 照片可能通过 Apple 系统服务按需下载媒体；拾晖本身不是独立的照片备份或云服务。

保存在设备上的记录

拾晖会在设备上保存待删除项与保留项的标识、清理日期和数量、文件大小估算总计，以及每组数量、自动播放等偏好，以恢复待删除列表、展示清理历史并记住你的选择。

主动分享时，App 可能在设备上生成临时导出文件。拾晖不会在自有服务器保存相册或清理记录副本。从设备删除 App 会移除这次安装的本地应用数据；系统的“卸载 App”保留数据功能或撤回相册权限，不等于删除应用数据。

删除与相册权限

左滑只会把项目加入待删除列表，不会立即从系统相册删除。只有在你预览并确认后，才会通过系统相册执行删除。

已删除的照片和视频通常在“最近删除”中保留 30 天。启用 iCloud 照片后，删除可能同步到你的其他设备。拾晖显示的文件大小估算不保证设备可用空间立即增加。

你可以在系统设置中缩小或撤回相册访问权限。撤回权限会停止后续访问，但不会删除系统相册、清除已经保存的应用记录，也不会撤销你此前确认的删除。

由你主动打开地图或分享

只有你选择打开地图时，拾晖才会将项目原有的位置坐标交给 Apple 地图。查看详情时不会自动请求地图图片。

分享会打开系统分享面板，由你选择接收方并决定是否发送。App 会在可取得时使用当前编辑后的版本；分享文件可能包含原有元数据。你选择的接收 App 或服务，会按其自身的数据处理方式接收内容。

当你联系支持时

支持通过下方公开的 GitHub Issue 页面提供。你创建 Issue 后，GitHub 和公开仓库的访问者可以看到你的 GitHub 账号名称、消息和附件。App 不会自动发送诊断报告或你的相册。

请勿发布照片、视频、精确位置或其他敏感信息。你可以使用 GitHub 提供的功能编辑或关闭 Issue。GitHub 会按照其条款和隐私声明处理相关信息；拾晖不承诺固定回复时限。
